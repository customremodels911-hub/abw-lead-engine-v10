# Tests

## Test 1 - Add, retain, and prioritize manual leads [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: manual lead intake, POST /api/leads, GET /api/leads, database persistence, score-based pipeline prioritization
Description: Verifies manual leads persist and the more actionable lead is surfaced ahead of a lower-score lead.

Steps:
1. Add lead "Basic Test" with no phone/email and project "General Remodeling"
2. Add lead "Priority Test" with phone "7575550101", email "priority@example.com", market "Virginia", and project "Electrical"
3. Refresh the page and inspect the Lead Inbox order

Expected: Both leads remain visible after refresh, and "Priority Test" appears above "Basic Test" because its lead score is higher.

## Test 2 - Scan public homeowner opportunities
Viewport: desktop
Covers: direct public opportunity workflow, POST /api/public-opportunities/scan, public Reddit ingestion, lead deduplication, GET /api/leads
Description: Verifies the operator can trigger a direct scan for public Charlotte, Hampton Roads, and New York City project requests and gets a visible result without disturbing the existing pipeline.

Steps:
1. Click "Scan Public Opportunities Now"
2. Inspect the result notice
3. Inspect the Lead Inbox

Expected: A notice beginning "Public scan complete:" is visible and any newly matched records appear with a public Reddit source for Charlotte, Hampton Roads, or New York City.
