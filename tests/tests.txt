## Test 1 - Add, plan, retain, and prioritize a real opportunity [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: manual lead intake, POST /api/leads, GET /api/leads, PUT /api/leads/:id/status, PUT /api/leads/:id/operator, database persistence, cash-conversion prioritization, weighted pipeline metrics, estimated-value tracking, next-action persistence, conversion-stage persistence, priority queue
Description: Verifies a real opportunity can be created, valued, assigned a next action, advanced in stage and retained as an operator-ready pipeline record.

Steps:
1. Add lead "Priority Test" with phone "7575550101", email "priority@example.com", market "Virginia", project "Electrical", and estimated value 7500
2. In its Lead Inbox row change status to "Qualified", set Next Action to "Call customer for site visit", and set a future Due date
3. Refresh the page and inspect Today's Priority Queue, the row, and pipeline statistics

Expected: "Priority Test" remains visible with value 7500, status "Qualified", the saved next action and due date, appears in Today's Priority Queue ahead of lower-stage opportunities, and both Active pipeline $ and Weighted pipeline $ reflect its value.
